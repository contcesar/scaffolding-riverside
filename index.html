Riverside scaffolding FAQ

How fast can you deliver scaffolding in Riverside?

It depends on the size of the job and our schedule. Smaller rentals can often go out within a few days. Call us for urgent jobs. Crews are available 24/7.

Do I need a permit for scaffolding in Riverside?

Scaffold on private property is often covered under the building permit for the work. Scaffold over a sidewalk or in the public right-of-way usually needs an encroachment permit from the City of Riverside. We can help with the drawings the city asks for.

Can you provide stamped engineering drawings?

Yes. We provide custom designs and stamped drawings for tall scaffolds, heavy loads, cantilevers, shoring and any job where the city, owner or general contractor requires them.

Do you rent scaffolding without labor?

Yes. You can rent material only, or have our crew handle delivery, setup, inspection and removal.

Do you work on homes as well as commercial sites?

Yes. We handle residential, commercial and industrial projects.
