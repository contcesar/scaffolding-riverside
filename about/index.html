About us

Riverside Scaffolding From a Team That Does It All

Scaffolding Riverside is run by Scaffold Solutions Unlimited, a California scaffold company with certified builders, engineering and material yards across the state.

Who we are

Scaffold Solutions Unlimited rents, sells, builds and engineers scaffolding for industrial, commercial and residential projects. Our crews are certified scaffold builders. Our engineers provide custom designs and stamped drawings.

We have locations in Whittier and Bakersfield and material yards across California. Riverside jobs are served from our Whittier yard. We cover Los Angeles, Orange, Riverside, San Bernardino, Kern and Fresno counties.

Our goal is simple. Give you a safe scaffold, on time, at a fair price.

Why contractors choose us

  • One company for everything. Material, labor and engineering, with one point of contact.
  • Safety first. Licensed, insured and OSHA-compliant. Every scaffold is inspected and tagged.
  • Fast response. Crews are available 24/7 for urgent jobs and changes.
  • Engineering in-house. Stamped drawings for permits, tall builds and heavy loads.
  • Every job size. From a single rolling tower to full building wraps.

How we work

Plan

We look at the site, the work and the loads. Then we pick the right scaffold and add engineering when needed.

Build

Our crew builds to plan, inspects the scaffold and tags it before your team steps on it.

Support

Need a change or a move? We adjust during the job, then dismantle and haul it away at the end.
